LATERALIS
Attackers don't jump to the crown jewels. They hop. We forecast the next hop and cut it before it lands.

A company network is a graph that changes every hour: machines and users are nodes, logins are edges. A temporal graph neural network (GAT + GRU) reads that evolving graph, predicts where a compromised attacker moves next, ranks full attack paths to the domain controller and database, and simulates the cheapest defensive cut.
