Why it matters

Most break-ins start on one ordinary laptop. The intruder signs in to one machine after another with stolen passwords, and each step looks like normal work until they reach the server that matters.

Questions

What people ask before they install Lateralis.

Ask on GitHub

Does Lateralis send my data anywhere?

No. Lateralis runs on your computer. When the assistants are on, names of people and computers are replaced with codes before any text leaves it.

Will Lateralis block anything on its own?

No. Every block or isolation waits for a person to approve it.

What does it need from my network?

The sign-in and connection records your systems already keep. Nothing is installed on staff laptops.

Can I try Lateralis without my own logs?

Yes. It ships with practice company networks, and the simulation runs in your browser.

Is there a Docker install of Lateralis?

Not yet. It is planned for version 1.0; until then use the commands above.
