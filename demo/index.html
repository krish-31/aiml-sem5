The problem

Most break-ins aren't one big hit. They're a quiet walk through your network.

It starts small

An attacker usually gets in through one ordinary laptop, often from a phishing email or a reused password.

Each step looks normal

They log in with stolen passwords, so every single move looks like a regular employee doing their job.

It's found too late

By the time anyone notices, the attacker has often reached the most important servers. Lateralis is built to catch them on the way.

How it works

Three steps. No security degree needed.

Lateralis treats your network like a map of who connects to what, and keeps watching how that map changes over time.

1

Connect your records

Point Lateralis at the login records your computers already keep. Nothing is installed on employee machines.

“Alice's laptop signed in to the file server at 9:14.”
2

It learns what normal looks like

It learns which people and computers usually talk to each other, and notices when someone starts moving in an unusual way.

“This laptop has never touched the web server before.”
3

It warns you and suggests a fix

When something looks like a break-in, it shows the computers the intruder is most likely to reach next and the simplest way to block them.

“Block this account from the domain controller.”

Questions

Good to know

Is Lateralis free?

Yes. It is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence, so you can use it, change it and share it at no cost.

Does it send my company's data anywhere?

No. Lateralis runs on your own computer or server. If you turn on the AI assistants, the names of people, computers and addresses are replaced with placeholders before any text leaves your machine.

Will it block things on its own?

No. Lateralis suggests what to do, but every action, like blocking an account or isolating a computer, waits for a person to approve it.

What does it need from my network?

Only the login and connection records your systems already keep. You don't install anything on employee laptops.

Do I need a powerful computer?

No. A normal laptop with 8 GB of memory is enough to try it. A graphics card is not required.

Can I try it without my own data?

Yes. Lateralis comes with sample company networks, and the online simulation lets you watch a full attack without installing anything.
